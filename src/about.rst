About LEXI
==========

The LEXI (Lunar Environment Heliospheric X-ray Imager) instrument will take global images of the
interaction of the solar wind and the Earth’s magnetic field. The instrument will be deployed and
operate from the lunar surface in 2025 through the NASA CLPS program. The project is a
multi-institute collaboration between Boston University, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Johns
Hopkins University, University of Miami, University of Kansas, and the University of Leicester in the
UK.

For more information, please visit the `LEXI home-page <https://sites.bu.edu/lexi/>`_.
